DEAD MAN'S BLUFF

"Everyone at this table is lying. Including you."

Four gunslingers sit around a blood-stained green felt table in 1881 frontier territory. A 24-card deck. Hidden hands. Audacious wagers on what lies across the table.

Raise the count, bid higher rank, or call LIAR! Caught bluffs and false accusations carry a permanent toll: two cards shot away forever. The last gambler standing takes the pot.

════ 📜 RULES OF THE SALOON 📜 ════

THE LIAR'S CODE

Read close, stranger. Ignorance gets you buried.

1. THE DECK: 24 cards: 4 each of A, K, Q, J, 10, 9. Rank order: A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9. Each player begins with 5 cards dealt face down.

2. THE BET: In turn, bid: "At least N cards of rank R exist across ALL hands." You may speak truth or bluff.

3. THE RAISE: Next player clockwise must RAISE (higher count of any rank, or same count of a higher rank) or CALL LIAR.

4. THE SHOWDOWN: On a call, all hands flip. If the bet was true, the caller loses 2 cards. If false, the bettor loses 2 cards. Lost cards are gone for good!

5. SURVIVAL: The round loser starts the next round. Reach 0 cards and you're dead. Last player standing wins.
